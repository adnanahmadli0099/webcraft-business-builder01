# Sitemap

- **Home (`index.html`)**: main introduction, primary call to action, featured services and contact route.
- **About (`about.html`)**: business background and values. Replace all prompts with verified facts.
- **Services (`services.html`)**: editable service cards. Remove services that do not apply.
- **Gallery (`gallery.html`)**: approved, authentic photos only.
- **Contact (`contact.html`)**: verified contact details, map directions and enquiry form.
- **Privacy (`privacy.html`)**: draft policy requiring review against actual data collection and applicable law.
- **Terms (`terms.html`)**: draft terms requiring business-specific review.
- **404 (`404.html`)**: friendly page for invalid routes.
